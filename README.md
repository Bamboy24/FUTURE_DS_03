# FUTURE_DS_03 - Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of Olist marketing funnel data to identify conversion 
performance, lead sources and business segment trends using Power BI.

## Key Insights
- 842 total deals won across 2017-2018
- Resellers dominate at 69.71% of all business types
- Home decor & health beauty are top performing segments
- Online medium is the highest converting lead type
- Strong upward trend in deals from 2017 to 2018

## Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query (Data Cleaning)

## Recommendations
1. Increase investment in online medium lead channels
2. Focus on home decor & health beauty segments
3. Develop targeted campaigns for reseller business type
4. Replicate cat behaviour profile targeting strategies
5. Explore underperforming segments for growth opportunities
